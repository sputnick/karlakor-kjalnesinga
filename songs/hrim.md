# Hrím

Aldinreitur rúðuklakans  
réttir þroskabráða leggi.  
Iða um frosna æðaveggi  
öfl og næring ljósvakans.  
Liljubros í ljórahrími  
lyftist yfir dauða hjarnið,  
eins og þegar blessað barnið  
biður guð í fögru rími.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** Single short quatrain, through-composed, both voice parts carrying the same words with no repeats or refrain.
- Transcribed from a cleanly typeset, unambiguous score (this edition is itself a scan of a published collection, 'Þrastabók 2007'/Ísalög, credited on the page). Iceland Music Information Centre's catalogue (shop.icelandmusic.is) confirms composer Friðrik Bjarnason and author Einar Benediktsson for this TTBB piece and gives 'Aldinreitur rúðu klakans' as its text sample, matching the opening line transcribed here, though it does not publish the rest of the text. The poem plays on the near-homonyms 'hrím' (rime/frost, the title) and 'rím' (rhyme, the closing word), which is intentional wordplay by Einar Benediktsson and not a transcription error.
