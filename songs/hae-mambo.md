# Hæ Mambó

Sem unglamb heim ég aftur sný  
úr orlofsferð til Napólí.  
Fríðari hvergi' en þar leit kvennafans  
þótt kynni ég hvorki þeirra dans né sönginn:  

Hæ Mambó, Mambó Ítalíanó,  
hæ Mambó, Mambó Ítalíanó,  
sí, sí, sí, þú ert Sikileyingur, o,  
gettu betur góða, gamall bóndi úr þingó.  

Hæ Mambó, þar er nú lífið, landi,  
hæ Mambó, og skáldin óteljandi  
yrkja ótal vísó,  
ástarlof og prísó til okkar daladísó.  
Svo ástarheitó er ekki nein í Mývatnssveitó,  
og heyrðu, mig vantar kaupakonó  
og kannski hef ég vonó,  
ef þú heldur heim með mér, þá  
heila drápu kveð ég þér.  

(Viðlag)  

Í haust er hættir slátt og dátt og kátt í réttó,  
dansinn stígum sæl og þéttó.  

Mambó, Ítalíanó, Mambó.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** Comedic novelty song, T.Solo carries the story with Tenor/Bass echoing 'Hæ Mambó...' underneath. Uses a 1st/2nd-ending repeat: verse 1 ('sí sí sí þú ert Sikileyingur') closes with ending 1 ('gettu betur góða...'); on the repeat, verse 2 ('þar er nú lífið, landi...') is sung and continues on into ending 2 and the rest of the song. The 'Hæ Mambó Mambó Ítalíanó' hook recurs several more times near the end (marked '(Viðlag)' here) with 'hó hó hó' scat filler omitted.
- Icelandic comic parody text by Loftur Guðmundsson to Bob Merrill's tune, úts. Páll Helgason. Upgrade pass: found the full text published on glatkistan.com (from Bogomil Font's recording) and, best of all, in the choir's own 2014 spring concert programme (karlakor.is, Vor2014.pdf, p.11), which lists this exact song with these credits and prints the words that were actually sung by this choir. Re-read the score at high zoom against both sources and fixed two genuine misreadings: 'Friðari' -> 'Fríðari' (the score does carry the acute accent, just easy to miss - 'fairer', not the non-word 'friðari') and 'skáldin ótalandi' -> 'skáldin óteljandi' ('countless poets', matching the score's 'ó-telj-and-i'); also added a missed 'Í' at 'Í haust er hættir...', visible as a trailing syllable at the end of the previous system. The choir's own programme's rendering of 'ástar, lof og prísó ... Dala-dísó' and the closing 'Mambó, Ítalíanó. / Mam-bó,' differ slightly from how the score itself lays the words out ('ástarlof og prísó ... daladísó' as compounds; ending 'Mambó, Ítalíanó, Mambó.'), so the score's own wording is kept for those spots as the primary source.
- **Proofread:** Form only: split three run-together poetic lines at their rhymes (line 1 after 'sný', line 2 after 'kvennafans', line 15 after 'vonó,'); wording unchanged.
