# Weep, O Mine Eyes

Weep, O mine eyes, and cease not,  
alas, these your springtides methinks increase not.  
O when, O when begin you  
to swell so high that I may drown me in you?  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** English Renaissance madrigal for 4 voices a cappella (from Bennet's 'Madrigalls to Fovre Voyces', 1599). The single short text is set imitatively so each voice enters at a different time repeating fragments ('Weep, O mine eyes, weep...'); the full continuous text is given once here rather than duplicating each voice's echoed entrance.
