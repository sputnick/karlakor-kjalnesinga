# Draumalandið

Ó, leyf mér þig að leiða  
til landsins fjalla heiða  
með sælu sumrin löng,  
þar angar blómabreiða,  
við blíðan fuglasöng.  

Þar aðeins yndi fann ég,  
þar aðeins við mig kann ég,  
þar batt mig tryggðaband;  
því þar er allt sem ann ég,  
það er mitt draumaland.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** Solo song ('Einsöngur, píanó og karlakór', radds. Páll Helgason) with two 5-line stanzas set to the identical melody throughout, printed as two stacked lyric lines under Solo/Tenor/Bassi. The closing line of each stanza ('við blíðan fuglasöng' / 'það er mitt draumaland') is echoed 2-3 times in the score for musical effect; written once per stanza here per the no-duplication rule.
- Upgraded to online-verified: found the published text on the LiederNet Archive (a recommended source for art-song texts), confirming the wording and giving the correct 5-line-per-stanza layout (the score's underlay had merged some of these poetic lines under the music). One real correction from the initial score-transcription: the final line is 'það er mitt draumaland' (it is my dreamland), not 'þar er mitt draumaland' (there is...) as first misread from the score.
- **Proofread:** 'blóma breiða' → 'blómabreiða' (compound; score underlay 'blóm-a-breið-a').
