# Ríðum sem fjandinn

Ríðum og ríðum og rekum yfir sandinn.  
Ríðum sem fjandinn.  
Sláum í gandinn,  
þannig skemmtir sér landinn.  

Ríðum og ríðum og rekum yfir sandinn.  
Ríðum sem fjandinn.  
Sláum í gandinn,  
þetta er stórkostleg reið.  

Það er fullt af bruggi í flöskunni  
og flatbrauðssneið í töskunni  
og glóð er enn í öskunni,  
við komum öskufullir heim.  
Skál!  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 3 set in the score, 3 written here.
- **Form:** Choral arrangement (radds. Páll Helgason). The score's underlay matches the published text (glatkistan.com) closely, including the two near-identical opening couplets and the closing 'ash/flatbread' verse. The score also prints a performance note ('2 bars of intro, often repeated, and then "SKÁL" dropped the first time through; alternatively modulate to F# with the passing chord C# in place of "SKÁL"') - this is a performance instruction, not sung text, and was excluded from the lyrics.
- Found the exact matching published text on glatkistan.com (an Icelandic music-history site), credited 'Lag/texti: höfundur ókunnur / Sigurður Þórarinsson'. That source ends the song with 'Í nótt!' where this score's arrangement instead ends with a shouted 'Skál!' (cheers) - a deliberate change fitting the arrangement's drinking-song ending (consistent with the score's own performance note about how to handle the word 'SKÁL'), so 'Skál!' was kept per the score rather than 'Í nótt!'.
