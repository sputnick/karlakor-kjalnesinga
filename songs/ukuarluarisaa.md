# Ukuarluarisaa

Ukuarluarisaa kusanannguaruni.  
Ajjaj, aajaj, ajjajaa.  

Arluarisaa qilerteqannguaruni.  
Ajjaj, jaaa, ajjajaa.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** One-page arrangement of the opening phrase of a traditional South Greenlandic narrative song ('Rejected bride'); only the incipit phrase and its vocable refrain are set (not the full narrative ballad).
