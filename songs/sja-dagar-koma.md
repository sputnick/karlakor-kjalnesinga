# Sjá dagar koma

Sjá, dagar koma, ár og aldir líða,  
og enginn stöðvar tímans þunga nið.  
Í djúpi andans duldir kraftar bíða.  
Hin dýpsta speki boðar líf og frið.  

Í þúsund ár bjó þjóð við nyrstu voga.  
Mót þrautum sínum gekk hún, djörf og sterk,  
í hennar kirkju helgar stjörnur loga,  
og hennar líf er eilíft kraftaverk.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** Andante, TTBB. Tenór Solo carries all the text throughout; Tenór I-II and Bassi I-II hum 'con bocca chiusa' (closed-mouth) under the solo the whole piece, so no separate choir text exists. Two 4-line stanzas, sung straight through with no repeats.
- Matches the published poem at snerpa.is (credited Sigurður Þórðarson / Davíð Stefánsson) almost verbatim. Two words kept per the score's own clear engraving (Íslensk tónverkamiðstöð edition, copyright Sigurður Þórðarson) rather than the online text: 'kirkju' (singular) where the online text has 'kirkjum' (plural), and 'helgarstjörnur' printed and hyphenated as one compound word ('helg-ar-stjörn-ur', no space, confirmed by zooming the page image) where the online text has two words 'helgar stjörnur'. PROOFREAD: "helgarstjörnur" (= weekend stars) is not what the poem means; the score's underlay is simply cramped at that point (it also runs "umgekkhún" together) and the online text has "helgar stjörnur" (holy stars), so the two-word form is used.
