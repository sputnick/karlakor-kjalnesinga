# Heillandi vor

Lýsast óðum langar nætur  
ljósið fyllir hvert eitt spor,  
dimman þverr og döggin grætur,  
dagsól veikum eykur þor.  
Út við fjallsins fögru rætur  
fjólan vex í klettaskor.  
Eins í okkar hjarta  
ómar vorið bjarta,  
ástarinnar unaðsljúfa vor.  

Anga fögru blómin bláu  
blíðlynd kvakar fuglahjörð,  
brosir sól við býli lágu,  
blikar dögg um grænan svörð.  
Blítt skal vagga barni smáu  
blessuð sólin heldur vörð.  
Milli grænna greina  
göngum vegu beina,  
gyllir sólin gróðursæla jörð.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** The published poem (words: Þorsteinn Sveinsson) is in two 9-line stanzas, given above in that order. The score's musical realization reorders/repeats this slightly for effect: the solo (first pass, in Ab) sings all of stanza 1 and then previews the opening three lines of stanza 2 ("Anga fögru blómin bláu...") as a tag; the full TTBB choir (second pass, transposed to Db, marked "unis") then sings the rest of stanza 2's own material ("Blikar dögg...jörð") and repeats those same three preview lines again at the equivalent spot. That extra repeat is the same text already given above and is not written out a third time; the wordless "Du-ru"/"Úú"/"Aaa" scat intro and backing vocalise are also omitted as filler.
- Confirmed against the published text at glatkistan.com and guitarparty.com's metadata (both list Lag: Óðinn G. Þórarinsson, Texti: Þorsteinn Sveinsson, matching the score). Kept the score's clearly engraved "dimman þverr" (definite, nominative subject "the darkness") where glatkistan.com has "dimma þverr" (indefinite) - both are grammatically valid and the score is unambiguous on this point.
