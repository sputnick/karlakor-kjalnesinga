# Kvöldljóð

Gengin er sól að grænum viði,  
golan mér strýkur létt um kinn.  
Ilmandi jörðin andar friði,  
unaðarkennd í sál mér inn.  

Yfir mér hvelfist bjartur, breiður,  
blikandi stjörnuhiminn skær.  
Skínandi, fagur, hár og heiður,  
horfir á lítið blóm sem grær.  

Brosir við augum yndisfögur  
almættisdýrðar veröldin.  
Bráðum mun aftur láð og lögur  
ljóma við árdagssólarskin.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 3 set in the score, 3 written here.
- **Form:** Three verses set to the same music (an 8+5+8-bar phrase with a written-out repeat of the last two phrases), printed as three parallel text lines (Tenór sóló, then echoed by the other voice parts on 'Oh' under verse 1's text, then all parts singing verses 2 and 3 in turn). Only the words of the single melodic pass are given; the repeated 'Oh' vocalise echo under verse 1 was not duplicated since it carries no text.
- Full text confirmed via two Morgunblaðið obituary quotations on timarit.is (1994 and 1999, both quoting this poem in full as a funeral verse) and via this choir's own 'Vor 2012' concert programme PDF (karlakor.is), which prints verse 3 directly. This corrected two misreadings from the initial score-only transcription: verse 2's 'fagur, hár og heiður' (three separate adjectives), not the guessed compound 'fagurhár', and verse 3's 'yndisfögur' and 'árdagssólarskin' (compounds), not 'yndislegur' and 'árdags sólarskin'.
- **Proofread:** no changes
