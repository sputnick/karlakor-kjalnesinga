# Nú legg ég augun aftur

Nú legg ég augun aftur,  
ó, Guð, þinn náðarkraftur  
mín veri vörn í nótt.  
Æ, virst mig að þér taka,  
mér yfir láttu vaka  
þinn engil svo ég sofi rótt.  

Ég fel í forsjá þína,  
Guð faðir, sálu mína  
því nú er komin nótt.  
Um ljósið lát mig dreyma  
og ljúfa engla geyma  
öll börnin þín svo blundi rótt.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** Two verses set strophically to the same P. C. Krossing melody: verse 1 is Sveinbjörn Egilsson's classic Icelandic rendering of Peter T. Foersom's 1813 Danish evening hymn ('Nu lukker sig mit øje'); verse 2, 'Ég fel í forsjá þína', is Matthías Jochumsson's companion verse, traditionally sung as verses 1-2 of this same children's evening prayer/hymn (nr. 430/510-511 in the Icelandic hymnal). No refrain, no repeats.
- Confirmed word-for-word via direct fetch of both kirkjan.is hymnal pages: 'Nú legg ég augun aftur' (#430: Peter T. Foersom 1813, þýð. Sveinbjörn Egilsson, Sb. 1871) and 'Ég fel í forsjá þína' (#431: Matthías Jochumsson, Sb. 1945), both set to Peter C. Krossing's 1820 tune ('Nu lukker sig mit øje'). These are technically two separate, consecutively numbered hymns, but this score (and Icelandic tradition generally) pairs them as verses 1-2 of one children's evening prayer, matching the transcription exactly (only very minor comma placement adjusted to match the published punctuation).
