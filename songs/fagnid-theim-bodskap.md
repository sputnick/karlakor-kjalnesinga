# Fagnið þeim boðskap

Frá himni Guðs í húmi um nótt kom heilagt englalið  
og flutti snauðum fjárhirðum Guðs fögnuð, náð og grið.  
Því borið var í Betlehem það barn er veitir frið.  

Ó, fagnið þeim boðskap sem ber, fagnið sem ber,  
ó, fagnið þeim boðskap sem ber.  

Þér ekki skuluð óttast hót, Guðs engill blíður tér,  
í dag er fæddur Frelsarinn af fríðri meyju hér,  
er bjarga mun frá böli og synd hinn blíði Immanúel.  

(Viðlag)  

Ó, fagnið allir, fagnið menn því fregn sú berst um geim,  
að sonur Guðs er sendur oss, er synda berum keim,  
að leiða oss um ljóssins stig til lífs að flytja heim.  

(Viðlag)  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 5 set in the score, 5 written here.
- **Form:** Icelandic translation (by Sigurður Pálsson) of the traditional English carol 'God Rest Ye Merry, Gentlemen', arranged for men's choir with solo (Raddsetning: Páll Helgason). Each of the three verses is sung by the Sóló line first (Tenor/Bass hum 'la/lall' behind it), then the whole choir joins for the shared refrain 'Ó, fagnið þeim boðskap sem ber, fagnið sem ber, ó, fagnið þeim boðskap sem ber.' (mirroring the English 'O tidings of comfort and joy'), written out once and marked '(Viðlag)' at its two later recurrences. A short wordless 'Humm' backing figure in the tenor under part of verse 2 was omitted as pure filler.
- Transcribed directly from a clean, clearly engraved score (not from OCR - read straight off the printed lyrics under the notes); web search was unavailable while working on this song (session web-search budget was exhausted), including a couple of direct Bing lookups for this exact translation that returned no results, so the Sigurður Pálsson text could not be checked against an independently published source such as a hymnal. Two words were less legible in the print and are best-effort readings: 'húmi' (verse 1, 'í húmi um nótt' = 'in the dusk of night') and 'fríðri' (verse 2, 'af fríðri meyju hér' = 'born of a fair maiden here') - both fit the grammar, meaning and the tune's note count well, but should be checked against the original print if available. Upgrade pass: found Sigurður Pálsson's full published translation on olisig.is (a choir's own lyric page). Both uncertain words were confirmed correct as read ('húmi', 'fríðri'). Three further wording corrections came from the published text: verse 2 'óttast þó' -> 'óttast hót' (an archaic intensifier, 'not fear in the least', misread on the score), 'blíður er' -> 'blíður tér' (the archaic verb 'téa', 'to show/grant', not the copula 'er'), and 'er bjargaður frá böli og synd' -> 'er bjarga mun frá böli og synd' (future 'will save', describing Immanúel as the one who will save, not 'is saved'); verse 3 'er syndum berum keim' -> 'er synda berum keim' (genitive 'taste of sin(s)', not dative).
