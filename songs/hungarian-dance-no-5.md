# Hungarian Dance No. 5

Ba ba ba ba, bam bam bam bam.  
Da ba da ba dam ba da.  

Hi-a-pa-pa-pa-pa-pa-pa-pa-pa, hi-a-pa-pa-pa-pa-pa-pa-pa-pa.  
Uh, uh, uh, uh, pa-pa-pa-pa-pam.  

BAM, BAM, BAM!  

## Notes

- **Form:** Purely instrumental piece (Brahms' Hungarian Dance No. 5, TTBB arrangement by Robert Sund, Schott 2017) with no real lyric text anywhere in either source PDF: every voice sings invented wordless scat/vocalise syllables throughout (Ba/Bam/Da/Doh/Dom/Pa/Uh/Hi-a-pa-pa etc., ending on a shouted "BAM, BAM, BAM!"). The syllables above are a short representative sample of each recurring motif in the order they appear, not an exhaustive transcription of every repeated bar (the piece is highly repetitive scat throughout, e.g. dozens of bars of "Ba ba ba ba").
- language set to 'zxx' (ISO 639-2 code for 'no linguistic content') since the sung syllables are nonsense vocalise, not real words in any language. Two identical scores exist in the sheet-music folder (an English-filed 'Hungarian Dance No. 5 - TTBB.pdf' and an Icelandic-filed 'Ungverskur Dans nr. 5...pdf'); both contain the exact same Schott edition/arrangement with the same vocalise text, so they were treated as one song, not a duplicate/extra song.
- **Proofread:** no changes
