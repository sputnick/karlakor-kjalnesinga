# Ver hjá mér, Herra

Ver hjá mér, Herra, dagur óðum dvín,  
ó, Drottinn, ég hef lengi saknað þín.  
Í æskuglaumnum gleymdi sál mín þér,  
í gleðidraumnum uggði' eg lítt að mér.  

En þegar loksins lækka tók mín sól,  
ég leita fór og spyrja: "Hvar er skjól?"  
En veröld gegndi: "Veika dauðans hey,  
þín von er fánýt, – Guð þú finnur ei".  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** Hymn 426 (Icelandic Sb. 426), TTBB arr. Þórður Sigurðarson. Score sets verses 1 and 2 with full text; a third statement of the melody is marked 'Bocca chiusa' (closed-mouth humming) with no words at all (dots under every note), so it is omitted from the lyrics as instructed for vocalise-only passages.
- Matthías Jochumsson's Icelandic version of Henry Francis Lyte's hymn 'Abide with Me' (tune 'Eventide' by W. H. Monk). Full 6-verse hymn text found on utfararstofa.is; only verses 1-2 are set with text in this score (matches the file name 'bæði erindi' = 'both verses'), so the remaining 4 published verses are left out.
