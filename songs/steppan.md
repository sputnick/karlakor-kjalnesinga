# Steppan

Steppan sig teygir  
miklu lengra en augað eygir,  
víðáttan endalaus er, sólin svíður,  
svalara þó er, fram á daginn líður.  

Kósakkar þeysa,  
láta hófagamma geysa,  
gleðjast á kvöldin yfir góðum vini,  
glasi af vodkablöndnu mánaskini.  

Hér vil ég eiga  
alla daga er veitast mega,  
dansa og syngja meðan bálið brennur,  
bræðralags okkar drekka skál!  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 3 set in the score, 3 written here.
- **Form:** Through-composed two-stave TTBB arrangement of a Russian folk tune. The main narrative text is carried by the lead voice; a second voice sings backing nonsense/vocalise syllables ('bamm babamm bamm', 'bam ba ra bam bammam ba rabam') simultaneously in several places, which are omitted as filler per instructions.
- Found the choir's own earlier concert programme (Vor 2012, karlakor.is), which prints this exact song's text with the same title and lyricist credit ('Rússneskt þjóðlag – Óskar Ingimarsson'); it confirms and clarifies the initial score transcription in full, including the word 'hófagamma' in 'láta hófagamma geysa', which had been uncertain from the scanned score alone.
