# Vor

Ljómar heimur logafagur,  
lífið fossar, hlær og grær.  
Nú er sól og sumardagur,  
söngvar óma fjær og nær.  
Vorsins englar vængjum blaka,  
vakir lífsins heilög þrá.  
Sumar glaðir svanir kvaka  
suður um heiðavötnin blá.  

Hvílir yfir hæðum öllum  
himnesk dýrð og Guðaró.  
Yfir jöklum, fram á fjöllum,  
fellir blærinn þokuskóg.  
Nú er gott að vaka, vaka,  
vera til og eiga þrá.  
Sumar glaðir svanir kvaka  
suður um heiðavötnin blá.  

Drekk ég glaður fjallafriðinn,  
fylli skálar sólskinsró.  
Teigar ljós við lækjarniðinn  
lítil rós í kletta tó.  
Sé ég fagra sýn til baka,  
sólareld og fjöllin blá.  
Nú er gott að vaka, vaka,  
vera til og eiga þrá.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 3 set in the score, 3 written here.
- **Form:** TTBB, subtitled with its first line 'Ljómar heimur logafagur'. Score stacks three full verses under the same music (numbered by row of lyrics). Verses share some end-couplets by design (verses 1-2 both end 'Sumar glaðir svanir kvaka / suður um heiðavötnin blá'; verse 2's third couplet 'Nú er gott að vaka, vaka / vera til og eiga þrá' recurs as verse 3's closing couplet) - these are the poem's own repeated lines, not voice-part or OCR duplication, so each verse is written out in full.
- A Firecrawl search located a 1994 Morgunblaðið obituary (timarit.is/page/1808330, behind a captcha so not directly fetchable) whose indexed snippet quotes 'Ljómar heimur logafagur lífið fossar, hlær og grær... (Friðrik Hansen)', independently confirming the opening line of this score transcription verbatim; the ismus.is/bragi entry for this poem (erindi/4969) also confirms the poem's existence and author but is JS-rendered and did not yield text via scrape. The score itself is a clean, clearly typeset edition (not a scan of a handwritten/old print), so the transcription is high-confidence. 'himneskdýrð' in the OCR hint was split back into the two words 'himnesk dýrð' (a tied melisma in the score, not one compound word) for correct Icelandic grammar. Upgrade pass: found the full poem (Friðrik Hansen, 'Vor') on Glatkistan, and two further reprints of it in Morgunblaðið (2013, 2016, via timarit.is), all three stanzas. These agree with the score almost everywhere, but disagree with each other and with the score on several word boundaries (Icelandic compounds vs. two words); since the score's own hyphenation is the deciding evidence, each case was checked by re-zooming the engraving: confirmed hyphens run continuously through 'Guðaró', 'þokuskóg' and (twice) 'heiðavötnin', so those are written as one word each (previously mis-split as two); by contrast 'kletta tó' is followed by a held-note underscore, not a hyphen, before 'tó', matching the score's own convention for a genuine word break elsewhere in the piece (e.g. 'lítil___rós'), so it stays two words despite the online sources' 'klettató'. 'sólskins ró' was changed to one word 'sólskinsró' to match all three online sources, since the score's own mark there is an ambiguous small dot rather than a clear hyphen or underscore. 'Teigar' (not 'Teygar') and 'logafagur' (one word, matching the score's own subtitle) were re-confirmed against the score and kept as transcribed, since two of the three online copies disagree with each other on 'Teigar/Teygar' and the score is unambiguous. Status upgraded to online-verified.
