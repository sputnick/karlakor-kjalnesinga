# Fuglinn sefur suðr' í mó

Fuglinn sefur suðr' í mó,  
sefur kisa' í værð og ró,  
sefur, sefur dúfan.  
Sofðu líka sætt og rótt,  
sofðu vært í alla nótt,  
sofðu litla ljúfan.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** Short one-stanza vögguvísa (lullaby), matches the score's music (a single short strophe).
- Corrected the title from the packet's/score's own 'Fuglin sefur suðri mó' to the standard spelling 'Fuglinn sefur suðrí mó' (missing 'n' on 'fuglinn', and 'suðrí' - a contraction of 'suður í' - rather than 'suðri', which is a different, unrelated word). Also corrected the packet's existing lyrics text, which had 'sefur kyrr í værð og ró' and 'sofðu litla ljúfran': the score's own underlay (both embedded text and vision OCR) actually reads 'kiss'' at that point, which independent published sources confirm is 'kisa'' (elided 'kisa í', kisa = kitten, a term of endearment), not 'kyrr'; and the correct final word is 'ljúfan', not 'ljúfran'.
- **Proofread:** Line 1 'suðrí mó' → 'suðr' í mó', matching the coordinator's title decision and the score underlay ('suðr' + 'í mó'); title field set to the same 'Fuglinn sefur suðr' í mó'.
