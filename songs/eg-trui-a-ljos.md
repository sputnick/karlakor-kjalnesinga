# Ég trúi á ljós

Ég trúi á ljós sem lýsir mér,  
á líf og sannleika  
og sigur þess sem sannast er  
og sættir mannanna,  
á afl sem stendur ætíð vörð  
um allt sem fagurt er,  
á Guð á himni, Guð á jörð  
og Guð í sjálfum mér.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** Short TTBB piece ('Karlakór', raddsetning Kjartan Sigurjónsson), music credited on the score only as 'Amerískt lag' (American tune); Hljóðsafn.is catalogues it as 'Ég trúi á ljós = Amazing grace' and Apple Music/Spotify credit Pétur Þórarinsson (1951-2007) as songwriter with the tune itself listed as 'Negrasálmur' (a Negro spiritual). The score prints this one 8-line poem as two 4-line strophes stacked under the same repeated melody (verse 1 = lines 1-4, verse 2 = lines 5-8); the online source (sol.heimsnet.is) prints it as one continuous 8-line stanza with no stanza break, which is how it is written here.

Corrected two words from the score-transcription against the online text: 'kærleika' -> 'sannleika' (love -> truth) and 'mannana' -> 'mannanna' (correct double-n genitive plural).
