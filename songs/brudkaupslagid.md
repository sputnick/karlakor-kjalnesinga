# Brúðkaupslagið

Við munum ganga inn kirkjugólfið,  
þá eilíf hamingja, basl og klúður,  
munum stíga brúðkaupsdansinn.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** TTBB chart (Úts. Kjartan Valdemarsson) built around a piano/solo vocal ('Einsöngur 2x') that carries the song's full melody and lyric; the choir sings almost entirely on a wordless 'ah' vocalise (omitted here as filler) and only voices these three short phrases, each a fragment of the full song (by the band Todmobile) confirmed online. 'Munum stíga brúðkaupsdansinn' is sung twice near the end (the second time with a ritardando); written once here since the words are identical both times.
- This is Todmobile's 'Brúðkaupslagið' (words: Andrea Gylfadóttir and Eyþór Arnalds; music: Þorvaldur Bjarni Þorvaldsson). The full song text (confirmed via glatkistan.com) has two full verses plus a bridge and is sung mostly by the soloist, e.g. 'Hvítur kjóllinn, slör sem dregur dilk á eftir sér... ég segi já ef hann einhvern tíma mun finna mig...'; only the three short phrases actually written into this TTBB choir excerpt are included here, matching the score's scope. The three lines here match the corresponding phrases in the full published lyric exactly.
