# Land míns föður

Land míns föður, landið mitt  
laugað bláum straumi  
eilíft vakir auglit þitt  
ofar tímans glaumi.  
Þetta auglit elskum vér,  
- Ævi vor á jörðu hér  
brot af þínu bergi er,  
blik af þínum draumi.  

Hvort sem krýnist þessi þjóð  
þyrnum eða rósum  
hennar sögur, hennar ljóð,  
hennar líf vér kjósum.  
Ein á hörpu ísa og báls  
aldaslag síns guðamáls  
æ hún leiki ung og frjáls  
undir norðurljósum.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** Score title page reads 'Landið mitt - Land míns föður -'; kept the packet's title 'Land míns föður' since it is the poem's actual/original title (also used by Tónlistarmiðstöð Íslands and ljóð.is) and 'Landið mitt' is used by a different, unrelated song already in this batch (id 158). Score sets both stanzas of the poem to the same melody as verse 1 and verse 2 printed under the staff; sung in that order, no refrain or repeats.
- Matched Jóhannes úr Kötlum's published poem on ljóð.is almost exactly. One word differs: ljóð.is prints '- Ævi vora á jörðu hér', but the score (verified directly from the printed music, one note per syllable) clearly sets 'ævi vor á jörðu hér' (one syllable fewer) — kept the score's 'vor' as sung. Also standardized 'ísa og báls' (no OCR errors there, matches both sources). check_song.py flags 'hennar sögur, hennar ljóð,' as a possible credit/performance marking (the word 'ljóð'); this is the genuine lyric word 'ljóð' meaning 'poems' inside the poem itself ('her stories, her poems'), not a credit line, so it was left in.
- **Proofread:** "þetta auglit" → "Þetta auglit" (capital after full stop, line 5); "norður ljósum" → "norðurljósum" (last line)
