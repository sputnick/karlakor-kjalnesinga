# Pílagrímakórinn

Nú hýrnar geð, ég sé heiminn í dalinn,  
í himindýrð lít ég bláfjallasalinn.  
Nú get ég staf minn geymt um stund,  
því gengið hef ég á drottins fund.  
Ég grét og bað, ég bað og grét,  
uns blíður drottinn líkn mér hét.  
Mín synd var ljót, þú leystir mig,  
öll ljóð og hljóð mín dýrka þig.  
Sá öðlast náð er þess iðrandi biður,  
og honum vís er hinn himneski friður,  
hann veit í þraut að leita lags,  
því lof' ég guð til hinsta dags.  
Halelúja! Halelúja!  
um eilíf ár, um eilíf ár.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** Through-composed (one continuous text); rehearsal numbers 1-5 mark sections, no verse/refrain structure other than the final 'Halelúja... um eilíf ár' which the score itself writes twice for a dynamic build p->cresc->ff; kept both since they are part of the notated melody, not a voice-part duplication.
- SECOND PASS: Icelandic singing translation of Wagner's Pilgrims' Chorus from Tannhäuser ('Beim letzten Abendstrahle'). Score title page reads 'Pílagrímasöngur / úr Tannhäuser / Richard Wagner' with no translator credited, but the opening 8 lines ('Nú hýrnar geð...drottins fund') are independently quoted, word-for-word identical, in two unrelated Morgunblaðið/Tíminn articles (1953 and 1999); the 1999 one explicitly attributes the translation to '(Jakob J. Smári)' - i.e. Jakob Jóhannesson Smári, a well-known Icelandic poet/translator - so that credit was used here. Both online quotations read 'lít ég' at the point this score's own underlay appears (on close zoomed reading) to say 'til ég'; given two independent, unrelated sources agree exactly, 'lít ég' (grammatically 'I behold') was adopted as the correct/published wording instead of the score's apparent 'til ég'. The remaining lines (from 'Nú get ég staf minn...' onward) were not found independently online, so they are transcribed directly from the (very legible, embedded-text) score. check_song.py flags 'öll ljóð og hljóð mín dýrka þig' as a possible credit marking (matches 'ljóð'); this is a genuine lyric line ('all my songs and sounds worship thee'), not a credit, so it was kept.
- **Proofread:** "óðlast" → "öðlast" (line 9; the score itself misprints "óðl-ast")
