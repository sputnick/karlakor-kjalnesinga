# Lítill drengur

Óðum steðjar að sá dagur,  
afmælið þitt kemur senn.  
Lítill drengur, ljós og fagur,  
lífsins skilning öðlast senn.  
Vildi ég að alltaf yrðir  
við áhyggjurnar laus sem nú,  
en allt fer hér á eina veginn:  
Í átt til foldar mjakast þú.  

Ég vildi geta verið hjá þér,  
veslings barnið mitt,  
umlukt þig með örmum mínum.  
Unir hver með sitt.  
Oft ég hugsa auðmjúkt til þín,  
einkum þegar húmar að;  
eins þótt fari óravegu,  
átt þú mér í hjartastað.  

Man ég munað slíkan,  
er morgun rann með daglegt stress,  
að ljúfur drengur lagði á sig  
lítið ferðalag til þess  
að koma í holu hlýja,  
höfgum pabba sínum hjá.  
Kúra sig í kotið hálsa,  
kærleiksorðið þurfti að fá.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 3 set in the score, 3 written here.
- **Form:** This is a well-known Icelandic song by Magnús Kjartansson (music) and Vilhjálmur Vilhjálmsson (lyrics), a father's message to a young son on his birthday. The TTBB choral setting in this score presents the 3 verses as a canon/round with the parts entering at staggered times and heavy vocalise filler ('Úú', 'dú dú dú', 'da da da da'), which is why the packet's noisy OCR of it looked incomprehensible. Fixed a grammar slip from the online source: 'áhyggjunar' -> 'áhyggjurnar'.
- IMPORTANT CORRECTION for the coordinator: my first pass at this file (before this song's title turned out to be a real, findable song) was a from-the-score transcription that tried to stitch together a single narrative out of the 4 overlapping/staggered voice parts across all 6 pages of this canon arrangement, and it went seriously wrong in the second half — it invented what looked like a 4th stanza ('Einka þér til eftirbreytni, alla betri menn en mig...') and several nonsense words ('kærleiksorðið', 'hálsá' as a place name, 'festi þinnar brotni tré') by misreading staggered repeats of the same 3 verses as new content. A web search this time found the actual published lyrics (Magnús Kjartansson/Vilhjálmur Vilhjálmsson, on snerpa.is and glatkistan.com), which is only 3 verses and matches the score's first ~30 bars almost exactly (including the 'kærleiksorðið þurfti að fá' line, which the earlier misreading had, in fact, gotten essentially right). This file now uses that verified 3-verse published text throughout instead of the earlier reconstruction. One word in verse 3 ('hálsa' in 'Kúra sig í kotið hálsa') is unclear in meaning even in the published source and was kept as printed rather than guessed. Proofread: 'kotið hálsa' is a poetic inversion of 'hálsakotið' (the crook of the neck, i.e. snuggling into dad's neck); it is correct as published, no longer unclear.
- **Proofread:** no text changes; added a note (see notes)
