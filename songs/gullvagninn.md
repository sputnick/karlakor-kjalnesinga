# Gullvagninn

Sendu nú vagninn þinn að sækja mig,  
sendu nú gullvagninn að sækja mig.  
Gættu mín, geymdu mig, gefðu mér friðinn,  
langt hef ég farið og mig langar heim.  

Lengi hef ég reikað þennan refilstig,  
rökkvar senn og þreytan er að buga mig.  
Hvar fæ ég að halla mínu höfði nú?  
Herra, enginn getur bjargað nema þú.  

(Viðlag)  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** TTBB octavo, úts. Þórir Baldursson. The opening verse/refrain is sung three times (start, middle, end) with identical words, marked '(Viðlag)' at its last recurrence. Between the 1st and 3rd statements the choir sings an extended 'já/hm' vocalise; hidden inside it, the baritone part alone carries the second real verse ('Lengi hef ég reikað...'), while the bass part simultaneously sings a different, shorter variant of its first two lines ('Líður þessi dagur senn og dimma fer, djúpir eru skuggarnir sem þrengja að mér') before both parts converge on 'Hvar fæ ég að halla mínu höfði nú? Herra, enginn getur bjargað nema þú.' The scat syllables 'Babb babb babb bara' and all 'já/hm/ó/úó' vocalise are omitted as filler.
- Icelandic-language cover ('Sendu nú vagninn þinn að sækja mig') of an American spiritual/gospel song, arranged by Þórir Baldursson; no translator is credited on the score. No published lyric text was found online within the available search budget, so transcribed from the score, which is clean, modern typeset and unambiguous. The bass part's alternate first couplet during the bridge ('Líður þessi dagur senn og dimma fer, djúpir eru skuggarnir sem þrengja að mér') is a genuine simultaneous alternate text sung by only one voice part; it is described here rather than inserted into the main lyric to avoid presenting two different simultaneous texts as sequential.
