# Hvítu mávar

Handan við hafdjúpin bláu,  
hugur minn dvelur hjá þér.  
Ég bið að þú komir og kyssir  
kvíðann úr hjarta mér.  

Hvítu mávar, segið þið honum  
að mitt hjarta slái aðeins fyrir hann.  

Hvítu mávar, segið þið honum  
að hann sé það allt sem ég í brjósti ann.  

Þótt þú færir burt ég hugsa enn sem áður,  
um okkar liðnu tíð er ég þig fann.  

(Viðlag)  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 4 set in the score, 4 written here.
- **Form:** Icelandic setting of the Norwegian song 'Hvite måker'. Form, confirmed against a published lyric page: verse ("Handan við hafdjúpin bláu..."), then the refrain sung with two different closing lines back to back (both kept, since the words differ), then a bridge ("Þótt þú færir burt..."), then the refrain sequence (both endings) repeats in full before ending back on the first refrain ending - that whole repeat is the same text already given above and is marked with a single line rather than written out again, per the score's own D.S. al Fine repeat structure.
- Confirmed the refrain and bridge wording against a published lyric page (icetones.se), which matches the score closely. Two places where the score's clearly engraved underlay differs from that one online source and the score was followed instead, since it is the choir's own performing text: "Ég bið að þú komir og kyssir" (score, 'I ask that you come and kiss') vs. the website's "Ég vil að þú komir og kyssir" ('I want...'); and "kvíðann úr hjarta mér" (score, correct accusative object of kyssir) vs. the website's "Kvíðan úr hjarta mér" (nominative, reads as an unconnected sentence fragment there).
- **Proofread:** Removed the comma between 'kyssir' and its object 'kvíðann' (verse 1).
