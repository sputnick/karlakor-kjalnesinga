# Söngkveðjan

Látum sönginn hvellan hljóma,  
helgum honum þetta kvöld.  
Látum hreinar raddir róma,  
ritað orð á sögu spjöld:  
Dýrast gaf oss djásn í heim  
drottning lista, söngsins hreim.  

Hjartans strengir hreinast titra,  
himinborinn viður söng.  
Eins og bjartir geislar glitra,  
glaðast eftir élin löng.  
Þannig lífgar lúna sál  
ljúfust indælt söngsins mál,  
ljúfast indælt söngsins mál.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** Clean modern octavo (© Karlakór Reykjavíkur), Allegro maestoso e marcato. The same publication prints Edvard Grieg's original Norwegian 'Sangerhilsen' (Sigvald Skavlan, 3 verses) on the facing page; this choir's part uses the Icelandic translation 'Söngkveðjan' by Benedikt Gröndal, which sets only verses 1 and 3 of the original (verse 2 is not translated/set), marked '1' and '3' in the score. Each verse's closing couplet is restated once musically; in verse 1 the restatement is word-for-word identical ('drottning lista, söngsins hreim') and is written only once here, while in verse 2 the two statements use different words ('ljúfust' then 'ljúfast indælt söngsins mál') and both are kept.
- No online text of Benedikt Gröndal's Icelandic translation could be found (Firecrawl search was rate-limited when tried); transcribed directly from the clean, professionally engraved score, which is highly legible throughout.
