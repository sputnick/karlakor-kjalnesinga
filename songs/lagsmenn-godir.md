# Lagsmenn góðir

Hæ, lagsmenn góðir, glösum klingið og glaðir skulum við vera.  
Um litla hríð við lifum hér og löng og erfið mörg mæðan er.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** Whole piece (polka tempo, canon-like) is built from one two-line strophe sung repeatedly by the different voices with staggered entries, tied together by 'Hæ' interjections and, before the final round, the transitional words 'En núna:'. No other text occurs. Wrote the strophe once per formatting rules; the 'Hæ'/'Hæ hæ' calls are vocalise-type interjections rather than separate lyric content and are omitted, as is the transitional 'En núna:'.
- Could not find this text published online (searches for Jakob Jóh. Smári + 'glösum klingið' and similar turned up nothing); transcribed directly from the score, which is clearly legible throughout both pages. 'Glösum klingið' = imperative 'clink [your] glasses' (klingja glösum), dative plural of 'glas' + 2nd person plural imperative of 'klingja' — grammatically correct old-fashioned Icelandic word order, kept as printed.
