# Son Guðs ertu með sanni

Son Guðs ertu með sanni,  
sonur Guðs, Jesús minn.  
Son Guðs, syndugum manni  
sonar arf skenktir þinn,  
son Guðs einn eingetinn.  

Syni Guðs syngi glaður  
sérhver lifandi maður,  
heiður í hvert eitt sinn.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** Hymn no. 80 in this songbook, set to a German tune ('þýskt lag'); the text is the 14th (final) stanza of Hallgrímur Pétursson's Passíusálmur 25. One 8-line stanza; the tune's opening phrase is used twice (for lines 1-2 and again for lines 3-5), so the score engraves two lyric rows stacked under the same notes for that part — not two separate verses.
- Confirmed against the Passíusálmar text (Hallgrímur Pétursson, public domain) at snerpa.is and against tonlist.adventistar.is's hymnal entry for the same song. The page's original OCR ('current_page_lyrics') had two real word-order errors that this corrects: 'Guðs sonur eingetinn' should be 'son Guðs einn eingetinn' (confirmed by the score's own pickup note 'son' before the next system, and by the Passíusálmar text), and 'Syngi Guði glaður' should be 'Syni Guðs syngi glaður'. Kept the score's own 'Jesús minn' (nominative) and 'sonar arf' (two words) rather than the original Passíusálmar's vocative 'Jesú minn' and compound 'sonararf', since the score clearly prints them that way and this songbook may use a slightly adapted congregational-singing text.
