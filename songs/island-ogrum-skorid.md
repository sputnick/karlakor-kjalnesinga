# Ísland ögrum skorið

Ísland ögrum skorið,  
ég vil nefna þig,  
sem á brjóstum borið  
og blessað hefur mig,  
fyrir skikkan skaparans.  
Vertu blessað, blessi þig,  
blessað nafnið hans.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** Score sets only the famous opening stanza of Eggert Ólafsson's longer poem, sung through once and then repeated in full with an echoed final line ('sem á brjóstum borið og blessað hefur mig' sung twice at the very end); the repeat is marked (Viðlag) rather than duplicated. Proofread: the (Viðlag) marker was removed, since a whole-song repeat is not written out or marked.
- Confirmed word for word (including 'vertu blessað, blessi þig, blessað nafnið hans') against afigamli.is, tonlist.adventistar.is (Aðventkirkjan hymnal) and kvak.is, all crediting the same lyricist/composer as the score. The full original poem continues with further stanzas ('Gleymt þér get ég aldrei, göfug...') which this score does not set, so they are left out.
- **Proofread:** removed trailing "(Viðlag)": the only stanza is simply repeated in full (a musical repeat, not a refrain), so no marker is needed
