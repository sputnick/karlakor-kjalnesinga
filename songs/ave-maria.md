# Ave María

Ave María, Ave María, Ave María.  
Gef þeim himnesk jólin.  

Ave María, Ave María,  
Ave María, frelsa þær frá illu.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** This short TTBB arrangement (Úts. Óskar Einarsson) sets only the refrain lines of Indriði Einarsson's longer poem 'Ave María', not the narrative lines that precede each refrain in the full poem. Only the top voice carries text; the other parts double the melody without separate lyrics.
- Web search of the full poem confirms the wording and order of the refrain lines exactly as engraved in the score ('...Ave María, Ave María, Ave María! Gef þeim himnesk jólin.' and '...Ave María, Ave María, Ave María! Frelsa þær frá illu.'). The score omits the intervening narrative lines of each stanza ('Hríf um eilífð oss frá heljar nauðum', 'Bið þinn son að vernda oss frá villu. Í veröld eru margir stígar hálir. Um eilífð vernda allar látnar sálir.'), so only the fragment actually sung is included here, per the score.
