# Ó, blessuð stund

Ó, blessuð stund, er burtu þokan líður  
sem blindar þessi dauðleg augu vor,  
en æðri dagur, dýrðarskær og blíður,  
með Drottins ljósi skín á öll vor spor.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 1 written here.
- **Form:** Single-page hymn excerpt (numbered '67.' in its source hymnal) setting only the first of the full hymn's 3 stanzas; no repeats or refrain. The complete hymn (kirkjan.is #652) has 2 further stanzas ('Ó, blessuð stund er sérhver rún er ráðin...' and 'Ó, Guð minn, vek þá hugsun mér í huga...') not set in this score, so they are left out here per the instructions.
- Confirmed via Firecrawl search against kirkjan.is (Þjóðkirkjan sálmabók #652, 'Trúarlífið — Endalok lífs og eilíft líf'): original text by Wilhelm A. Wexels (1841, Danish/Norwegian, 'Tænk, når engang den tåge er forsvunden'), Icelandic translation by Matthías Jochumsson (Sb. 1886), music by Andreas P. Berggreen (1856). The published wording corrects one word misread from the score: 'sem blindar þessi dauðleg augu vor' (blindar = blinds), not 'sem bindur' as first read from the engraving — an easy misread given how similar the two words look in print; the online source is authoritative here per the standing rule to trust online wording.
