# Færðu mér jólin þín

Færðu okkur jólin þín.  

Við fögnum þér enn, friðsæla nótt,  
færðu okkur jólin þín.  

Úú, úú, úú, ákefð bærir hjörtu.  

Allt svo undurbjart, allt ljóma fær,  
dagur rís af draumblárri höfn svo skær.  
Farnist þér vel, friðsæla nótt,  
fölna senn ljósin þín.  
Þú gafst okkur þó góðsemdar nótt,  
gleðileg jólin þín, gleðileg jólin þín.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 4 set in the score, 4 written here.
- **Form:** Engraved TTBB choir arrangement (Kórútsetning: Óskar Einarsson) of Gunnar Þórðarson's song, text by Bragi Valdimar Skúlason. The arrangement changes the original 'mér' (me) to 'okkur' (us) throughout, fitting a choir singing together rather than a solo voice; the title is kept as printed on the score/as the song is known. Section A ('Við fögnum þér enn...færðu okkur jólin þín', bars 6-21) is written with a 1st/2nd-ending repeat, i.e. sung twice with identical words - written once here. The 'Úú, úú, úú, ákefð bærir hjörtu' section (bars 26-34) is headed 'Má sleppa' ('may be omitted') in the score, i.e. it is an optional ad-lib passage some performances can cut. The final cadence 'gleðileg jólin þín, gleðileg jólin þín' is genuinely written twice in a row in the score (not a large-scale repeat), so both are kept.
