# Röndótta mær

Röndótta mær, má ég koma þér örlítið nær?  
Má ég setjast hjá þér? Nei, nei, nei, nei, nei, nei.  
Ó, röndótta fljóð, viltu vera mér örlítið góð?  
Má ég vera með þér? Nei, nei, nei, nei, nei, nei.  

Af mannkostum og mennt ég mikið hef,  
mér Hermann hefur kennt, og Móra vek.  
Nú ég skelli mér í ham og heiminn skek,  
af Hermanni ég nam, og Móra vek.  

Ég dansa Óla Skansa með glans,  
ég spela fiðlu Hansa, langspilið á.  
Ég syngja kann og kvæðin man,  
af öðrum mönnum ber.  

Ó, röndótta mær...  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 3 set in the score, 3 written here.
- **Form:** Modern engraved score (Tölvusett 2017, Daði Þór Einarsson), so very legible. The 'Röndótta mær...nei nei nei nei nei nei' verse is echoed by a second voice singing only 'Hún sagði' as a lead-in to the shared 'nei' response, and that same verse's second half has a harmony-voice variant ('leggjast með þér' instead of the lead voice's 'Má ég vera með þér') - both are backing-vocal doublings of the lead melody's text and were omitted per the no-duplication rule (noted here instead). Likewise in the 'Óla Skans' section the lead voice sings actual scat syllables ('Dú-rú rú, dú-rú rú') while the harmony voice simultaneously sings real words ('Ég syngja kann og kvæðin man'); kept the real words and dropped the scat syllables since they are not the only text sung there. The song ends with a D.S. al Coda: it repeats from the opening 'Röndótta mær' verse through 'af öðrum mönnum ber', then jumps to a short Coda that is just 'Ó, röndótta mær...' - represented as one pass through the verse/bridge/dance section followed by that final Coda line, rather than writing the repeated verse out twice.
- Song by Jakob (Frímann) Magnússon, performed solo (1976, album 'Horft í roðann') and later by Karlakórinn Fóstbræður with Stuðmenn (1998); this score is the choir arrangement (úts. Magnús Ingimarsson). Web search (Firecrawl) turned up only short snippets, not a full lyrics page (Reddit, which had a full quoted excerpt, could not be fetched - it is blocked for scraping). The confirmed snippet 'Röndótta mær, má ég koma þér örlítið nær? Má ég setjast hjá þér? ... Má ég vera með þér? Hún sagði nei, nei, nei, nei, nei, nei' matches this transcription exactly. The rest of the song (from 'Af mannkostum...' onward) was not found online and is transcribed directly from the (very legible) score. Upgrade pass: a Firecrawl search surfaced a second-hand quoted excerpt of the 'Af mannkostum' verse in the same blocked Reddit thread's search snippet; it confirmed 'og mikið hef' and 'og Móra vek' as printed (the snippet's own rendering, 'mikla'/'Moravek', looked like a paraphrase, not the score's wording), so those were kept as already transcribed. It also prompted a re-zoom of the score, which caught a real transcription slip: bar 33-34 clearly reads 'Óla Skans-a með glans' (three syllables), not 'Óla Skans' með glans' as first transcribed; fixed. Proofread pass: the 'Af mannkostum' section carries two underlay lines (sung on the first pass and after the D.S.); both couplets are now written out, one after the other, in stanza 2.
