# Nú hnígur sól / Ég krýp og faðma

Nú hnígur sól að sævar barmi, sígur húm á þreytta jörð.  
Nú blikar dögg á blómahvarmi, blundar þögul fuglahjörð.  
Í hljóðrar nætur ástarörmum,  
allir fá hvíld frá dagsins hörmum.  

Ég krýp og faðma fótskör þína, frelsari minn, á bænarstund.  
Ég legg sem barnið bresti mína, bróðir, í þína líknarmund.  
Ég hafna auðs og hefðarvöldum,  
hyl mig í þínum kærleiksöldum.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** Two independent Icelandic hymn texts ('Nú hnígur sól' and 'Ég krýp og faðma', by two different authors) both set to the same well-known Bortniansky melody, printed as two stacked lyric lines throughout a single through-composed setting; corrected the title's word order to match the score's own header 'Nú hnígur sól / Ég krýp og faðma' (the packet had the two halves reversed).
- Transcribed directly from a very clean, modern, fully legible engraving (endurskrifað 2016, Daði Þór Einarsson); no OCR ambiguity of note. Upgrade pass: Firecrawl searches found both hymns published independently - 'Nú hnígur sól' on mbl.is's Ljóðabanki and timarit.is (credited to Axel Guðmundsson, matching the score), 'Ég krýp og faðma' on Aðventkirkjan's hymnal site (Höfundur texta: Guðmundur Geirdal, also matching the score) and Karlakór Akureyrar-Geysir's own repertoire list; also cross-checked against another men's choir's own recording of this exact combined piece (Karlakór Selfoss, on paroles-musique.com and lyrics.com). All sources confirmed the score's stanza selection and resolved three small transcription slips: 'breytta jörð' should be 'þreytta jörð' (weary, not changed), and the previously flagged awkward phrase is resolved as 'Í hljóðrar nætur ástarörmum' (not 'Ég hljóðra...') and 'bróðir, í þína líknarmund' (not 'bróðir þína líknarmund') - both were misreadings of the same short word ('Í'/'í') rather than a deliberate score variant. A Neskirkja funeral-service page separately credits the two texts' authors: 'Nú hnígur sól að sævar barmi - Axel Guðmundsson / Bortniansky' and 'Ég krýp og faðma - Guðm. Geirdal / Bortnianski' (schema has no field for two separate lyricists on one combined piece, so not added to the lyricist field). Upgraded to online-verified.
