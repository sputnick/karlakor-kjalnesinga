# Heims um ból

Heims um ból helg eru jól,  
signuð mær son Guðs ól,  
frelsun mannanna, frelsisins lind,  
frumglæði ljóssins, en gjörvöll mannkind  
meinvill í myrkrunum lá.  

Heimi í hátíð er ný,  
himneskt ljós lýsir ský,  
liggur í jötunni lávarður heims,  
lifandi brunnur hins andlega seims,  
konungur lífs vors og ljóss.  

Heyra má himnum í frá  
englasöng: „Allelújá.“  
Friður á jörðu því faðirinn er  
fús þeim að líkna sem tilreiðir sér  
samastað syninum hjá.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 1 set in the score, 3 written here.
- **Form:** This is not a normal congregational setting of the hymn: it is a wordless choral-backing arrangement ('Transcr: Óskar Einarsson', subtitled 'Eins og á [diski] með Heru, Ilmur af jólum II' - i.e. transcribed from a recording, arranged for men's voices to accompany a soloist). Nearly the whole piece is marked to be hummed/vocalised, not sung as words - the tenor line is explicitly marked 'Melody ekki syngja' (do not sing the melody) over long stretches, and the sung syllables given throughout are wordless fillers ('Úú', 'Aa', 'Hmm'). The only real words set anywhere in the four-page arrangement are 'Sam-a-stað syn-in-um...hjá' near the end, which is exactly the closing line of the hymn's third verse. Per the coordinator's explicit instruction, the singers want the complete public-domain hymn text shown (not just the sung fragment), so `lyrics` here holds all three verses of 'Heims um ból' (words: Sveinbjörn Egilsson, 1849, to Franz Gruber's 'Stille Nacht'/'Silent Night' tune) from the Icelandic hymnal, even though this specific choir arrangement only voices the final five words of it; the choir's other parts hum/vocalise under a soloist for the rest.
