# Ó helga nótt

Föllum á kné, nú fagna himins englar,  
frá barnsins jötu blessun streymir,  
blítt og hljótt til þín.  
Ó, helga nótt, ó, heilaga nótt.  

Föllum á kné, nú fagna himins englar,  
hjá lágum stalli lífsins kyndill,  
ljómafagur skín.  
Ó, helga nótt, ó, heilaga nótt.  

## Notes

- **Stanzas:** 2 set in the score, 2 written here.
- **Form:** Icelandic (Sigurður Björnsson) translation of Adolphe Adam's 'Cantique de Noël' ('O Holy Night'). Three arrangements of this song were supplied for the choir; the clearest, a one-page 'Kórpartur' (choir part only, arr. Páll Helgason), shows that the men's choir sings only the refrain/chorus twice, with the same opening and closing lines both times ('Föllum á kné, nú fagna himins englar' ... 'Ó, helga nótt, ó, heilaga nótt') and a different middle couplet each time (verse 1: 'frá barnsins jötu...'; verse 2: 'hjá lágum stalli...'), corresponding to the chorus after verses 1 and 2 of the carol. The full solo verses of the carol are carried by a soloist and are not part of this choir-part excerpt, so they are not included here. The other two supplied scores (an Eyþór Ingi-style arrangement and a 'KÓRAR' edition where the choir mostly hums 'úú'/'aa' under a soloist) set the same choir text where words are present.
- Confirmed via Firecrawl search: olisig.is publishes this exact excerpt under the title 'Ó, helga nótt - (kórviðlag)' (choir refrain) with the same translator credit, Sigurður Björnsson, and identical wording for both verses (also independently matched by songskolimariu.is and other lyric sites for the full song). One correction from the initial score reading: the second verse's third line is 'ljómafagur skín' (one compound word), not 'ljóma, fagurt skín' as first read from the engraving.
